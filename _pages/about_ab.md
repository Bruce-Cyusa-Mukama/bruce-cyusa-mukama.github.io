["The Art of Fingerstyle Guitar Arrangements (Volume 1)"]() is an ongoing project. Fingerstyle is the art of playing guitar by using all of using your 10 fingers to simultaneously play the melody, the harmony, and the bass of a song. For this pupose, the notes and chords of the song must be (re)arranged to allow a solo play or to transpose the song into a different music style. Fingerstyle guitar enables deeper musical expression, but it also requires good string picking technique and a mastery of music theory. As an original tutorial, this ebook shall be a collection of (highly) visual analyses and explanations of how I created my best fingerstyle guitar arrangements. It shall be similar to the best digital concept/artbooks, but for popular guitar music. It shall also cover the use of opensource software with affordable accoustic & electric instruments, in its appendixes.
