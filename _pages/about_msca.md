["Curriculum semi-supervised segmentation of medical images using predicted bounding boxes"](https://espace.etsmtl.ca/id/eprint/2945/) is my Master's thesis. It focusses on alleviating the need of large labelled datasets when learning medical image segmentation, because they are hard to obtain. The proposed method consists in exploiting domain knowledge, about the organ of interest (e.g., the prostate, a brain region) to directly constrain the predicted segmentation masks. Specifically, unlabeled images are exploited in a semisupervised curriculum context by using an auxiliary neural network to predict the bounding boxes around the organs in the unlabeled images. These predicted bounding boxes are then used to constrain the size, the global location and the topology of the corresponding predicted segmentation masks.
