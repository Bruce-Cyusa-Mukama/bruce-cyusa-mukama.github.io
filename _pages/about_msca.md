[This is my 2021 Master's thesis](https://espace.etsmtl.ca/id/eprint/2945/), is written in french, it focusses on alleviating the need of large labelled datasets for learning medical image segmentation, which are hard to obtain. The proposed method consists in exploiting domain knowledge, about the organ of interest (e.g., the prostate, a brain region), to constrain directly the predicted segmentations. Specifically, we exploit unlabeled images, in a semisupervised curriculum context, by using an auxiliary neural network to predict the bounding boxes of the organs that unlabeled images may contain. These predicted bounding boxes are then used to constrain the size, the global location and the topology of the corresponding predicted segmentations.
