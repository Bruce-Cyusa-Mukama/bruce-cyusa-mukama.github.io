["Robust Uncertainty Quantification Algorithms for Perception"](https://hal.science/hal-05049241/document) is my PhD dissertation, it focusses on developping novel rigorous and robust distribution-free uncertainty quantification methods for generic (black box) percetion models. The proposed methods are mainly based on conformal prediction, a finite sample, post-hoc, and distribution free uncertainty quantification (UQ) framework. The showcased applications mainly focus on robot-vision tasks, such as semantic image segmentation, multiple object detection tracking, but these UQ methods can be easily applied to other settings such as sound-based perception. This research project also involved multiple theoretical contributions including the use of copulas to prioritize the heterogeneous subtasks of object detection (regression and classification) and the use of additional calibration samples to show that finite size joint coverage guarantees can be acheived with estimated copulas.
