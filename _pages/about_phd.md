["Robust Uncertainty Quantification Algorithms for Perception"](https://hal.science/hal-05049241/document) is my PhD dissertation, it is written in french and focusses on developping novel rigorous and robust distribution-free uncertainty quantification methods for generic (i.e., black box) percetion models. The proposed methods are mainly based on conformal prediction, a finite sample, post-hoc, and distribution free uncertainty quantification (UQ) framework. The showcased applications mainly focus on robot-vision tasks, such as semantic image segmentation, multiple object detection tracking, but they can be readily applied to other settings such as sound-based perception. These methods also involve multiple theoretical contributions including the use of copulas to prioritize the heterogeneous subtasks of object detection (multivariate bounding box regression and object classification) and the use of an additional calibration sample to show that finite size joint guarantes can be acheived with copulas.
