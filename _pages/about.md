## Short Bio
I am a guitarist and a research scientist. Prior to this, I was trained as a control engineer at UTT (Université de Technologie de Troyes) and as a computer vision scientist at ÉTS (École de Technologie Supérieure de Montréal). Afterwards, I completed a computer science PhD program at UTC (Université de Technologie de Compiègne) and specialized in the development of rigorous and robust uncertainty quantification algorithms for machine learning. The main application was computer vision. Nowadays my research topics also include robotic systems and vision-language-action models. Beyond this profound and enduring fascination with problem solving, automation, and safety; my research is motivated by the need of intelligent systems that can safely assist or collaborate with humans to complete complex tasks. 

Regarding my guitar hobby, I mainly analyze, transcribe, and record guitar covers: fingerstyle guitar arrangements of popular songs. Occasionally, I also write original guitar songs and perform them for small crowds. This website briefly presents my scientific and artistic works. If you have any questions or would like to discuss potential opportunities, feel free to reach out using the details at the bottom of this page.

## Research Interests
Although I find joy in solving any problem that is related to intelligent systems, I am mostly knowledgeable in the following domains:
- Trustworthy Artificial Intelligence, 
- Machine Learning for Robotics,
- Computer Vision for Robotics.
