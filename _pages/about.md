## Short Bio
I am a guitarist and a research scientist. Prior to this, I was trained as a control engineer (M.Eng.) at UTT (Université de Technologie de Troyes) and as a computer vision scientist (M.A.Sc.) at ÉTS (École de Technologie Supérieure de Montréal). Afterwards, I completed a computer science PhD program at UTC (Université de Technologie de Compiègne) and specialized in the development of rigorous and robust uncertainty quantification methods for machine learning algorithms and computer vision. Nowadays, my research also extends to robot learning. 

Beyond this profound and enduring fascination with problem solving, automation, and safety; my research is motivated by the need of intelligent systems that can safely assist or collaborate with humans to complete complex tasks. Regarding my guitar hobby, I mainly analyze, transcribe, and record guitar covers (fingerstyle guitar arrangements) of new popular songs, with a personal touch. Occasionally, I also write original guitar songs and get to perform them for small crowds. This website presents my scientific and artistic works. To contact me, please find my details at the bottom of this page.

## Research Interests
Although I find joy in solving any problem that is related to intelligent systems, I am mostly knowledgeable in the following domains:
- Trustworthy Artificial Intelligence, 
- Machine Learning for Robotics,
- Computer Vision for Robotics.
